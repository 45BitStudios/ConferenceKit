---
name: find-attendee
description: Looks up people who opted into the attendee directory. Use for who is attending or a name in the directory. Not for a speaker's sessions, contact details, or sending a message.
---

## When this applies

An attendee, or the directory as a whole. A speaker on the schedule is a different question. When they ask when someone is speaking, use the session tools, not this one. If a name could be either a speaker or an attendee, ask which they mean.

## What you need

A name, a role, or a company. With no name, list who is in the directory. If several people match, name them and ask which one. Do not guess.

## Steps

Use the attendee directory lookup. Say only the names and roles it returns. If the person is not listed, say they are not in the directory. Do not invent an email, a phone number, or a social link.

This lookup does not return contact fields. If they ask for an email or a phone, do not read one from it. Public contact fields can be shown in the app only after the device is unlocked and they confirm. If they do not confirm, say that nothing was shown.

## Messages

There is no tool that delivers a message to another attendee. If they ask to text someone, tell them to open Messages in the app. Messages there stay on this device and are not delivered. Do not say a message was sent. Cancelling a draft in the app leaves it unsent.

## When a tool does not run

The directory list in the app still works on the device. Say that the attendee lookup did not run and point them there. Do not claim a server or a connection was reached. This skill is optional. It does not change someone's privacy settings, and it does not undo a block or a report.
