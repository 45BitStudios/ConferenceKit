---
name: export-notes
description: Exports the person's notes when they ask for a copy of all of them. Not for adding one note or reading a single session back.
---

## When this applies

They asked to export, share, or copy their notes. Do not export only because another request mentioned notes.

## What you need

Nothing beyond that request. The export covers the notes stored for this conference.

## Steps

Use export notes. The result is Markdown grouped by conference day and session. If there are no notes, say so. Do not invent a file or a sentence they did not write.

Do not claim the file was sent to another person. A share sheet in the app is a separate step. If they cancel that share, the notes stay where they were.

## When a tool does not run

Tell them to open My Notes from Info in the app and export there. Notes remain on this device. This skill is optional. It does not roll back an export that already finished, and it must not claim a connection to a server.
