---
name: find-attendee
description: Looks up a person in the conference attendee directory. Use when someone asks who is attending, asks for an attendee by name, or asks for a public email. Not for speakers on the schedule, and not for sending a message.
---

Use the attendee lookup tool. Say only the fields it returns.

If the tool does not list the person, say they are not in the directory. Do not guess an email, a phone number, or a social link.

Do not claim a message was sent. Messaging in this app stays on device until a conference service is connected.
